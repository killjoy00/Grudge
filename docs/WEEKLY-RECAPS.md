# Weekly recaps

The homepage is the current-week surface. Once a regular-season week is settled,
its recap appears there until the next week becomes the current story.

Historical recaps live under **Standings**:

1. season standings
2. weekly recaps for that season
3. the season/year picker

Archived recap URLs are `/standings/recaps/{season}/{week}`. The archive begins
in 2018 because earlier commissioner history has season totals and playoff
results but not enough week-by-week data to reconstruct a recap faithfully.

## Email: notable pickups

The weekly email includes a **Pickups that paid off** block when a player:

- was an explicit `ADD` in an executed `WAIVER` or `FREEAGENT` transaction for
  the recap scoring week; and
- scored **more than 10 fantasy points** in that same scoring week.

Failed claims, trades and lineup-only roster moves are excluded. Each callout
shows the player's position, acquiring fantasy team, actual and projected
points when available, whether the player was started, and FAAB spent when the
bid was greater than zero.

The section is omitted entirely in weeks with no qualifying pickups.
